---
title: "BeaconClaw documentation"
description: "Fleet visibility for self-hosted AI agents."
---

BeaconClaw shows you which agents you run, whether each one is healthy, and who looks after it.

A small command-line program runs beside each agent and reports its health over an outbound connection. Nothing connects in to the machine.
