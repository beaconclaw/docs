title: "BeaconClaw documentation"
description: "Fleet visibility for self-hosted AI agents."
---

BeaconClaw shows you which agents you run, whether each one is healthy, and who looks after it.

A small command-line program, the BeaconClaw CLI, runs beside each agent and reports its health over an outbound connection. Nothing connects in to the machine. The app keeps the history: when each agent was up, what went wrong and for how long, and who was told.

It supports [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) and [Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent).
